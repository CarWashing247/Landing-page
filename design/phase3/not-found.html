  • Renders inside the real locale shell: the shared Header, Footer and skip link. Vietnamese at unprefixed URLs, English under /en.
  • One primary action only; the suggested pages below carry the rest, so Contact is not offered twice.
  • Exactly one h1. The eyebrow carries the code; the heading is the existing catalog sentence (notFound.message). The primary action is the existing notFound.backHome.

  • The response stays HTTP 404 with noindex. No metadata beyond the brand title; no canonical, hreflang or JSON-LD.
